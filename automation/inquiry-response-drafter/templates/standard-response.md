# Standard Inquiry Response Template

## Objective

Create a professional first-response draft for a high-end rental inquiry while avoiding unverified promises.

## Required checks before sending

1. Confirm current availability.
2. Confirm current rent, management fees, deposit/key money, and other initial costs.
3. Confirm furnishing status.
4. Confirm permitted lease type and term.
5. Confirm applicant eligibility and screening requirements.
6. Confirm proposed viewing dates with the property/owner side.
7. Confirm whether English-language support or English documents are actually available.

## English Template

**Subject:** Re: Your Rental Inquiry – [Property / Area]

Dear [Customer Name],

Thank you for contacting us regarding your apartment search in Tokyo.

We understand that you are looking for [requirements] with a target move-in date of [move-in date] and a budget of approximately [budget].

Regarding [property name], we will first confirm the latest availability and lease conditions. As availability and terms may change, we would prefer to verify the current information before making any commitment.

We can also review comparable properties that may suit your requirements. If you could share your preferred viewing dates and any additional priorities, such as parking, pet requirements, furniture, school access, or commute preferences, we can narrow the options accordingly.

Once the latest information has been confirmed, we will provide you with the available options and possible viewing arrangements.

Best regards,
[Staff Name]
[Company]

## Internal Review Flags

Before sending, flag any statement involving:

- availability
- price or fees
- application acceptance
- screening outcome
- move-in guarantee
- furnishing
- pets
- parking
- corporate lease acceptance
- foreign applicant acceptance
- English-language contract availability

These items must be verified against current source information.
